# ACTINV P11 — covariance, sensitivities and uncertainty reporting

**Roadmap row:** P11 (first phase of the v1.0 milestone). **Opened:** after the verified P10 close. **Time box:**
three calendar days plus one checkpointed corpus scan. **External acts:** the principal's. This protocol covers
ENDF-6 MF=33 neutron cross-section covariance, spectrum collapse, local first-order response sensitivities and
uncertainty reporting. It also exposes the already-vendored CRAM-48 coefficients and closes the numerical-method
budget routed here from `docs/PARKING.md`. Clearance/waste indices, ICRP coefficients, table re-verification,
fuzzing, FNG/ITER validation, v1.0 tagging/publication and all other P12 work remain out of scope.

MF=33 is only one component of a complete uncertainty assessment. P11 does not claim uncertainty for decay data,
fission or product yields, the incident flux, material composition, response coefficients, resonance parameters in
MF=32, radionuclide-production covariance in MF=40, or model discrepancy. Every report must call its numerical band
an **MF=33 nuclear-data band**, list uncovered activation rows, and keep the separately estimated CRAM numerical
bound visible.

## Normative covariance and wire-format choices

1. `actinv-data` owns strict MF=33 parsing and deterministic covariance-sidecar construction. Production code may
   not import the Python controls or require NJOY. The independent Python parser and NJOY ERRORR are controls only.
2. The CLI contract is
   `actinv build-covariance INPUT ACTIVATION.npz OUTPUT.cov.npz [--workers N] [--cache DIR]`. `INPUT` follows the
   deterministic file-order and source-mutation rules of `build-library`. The activation index's target identity and
   source SHA-256 must match every covariance source before a target is accepted. Only neutron libraries are valid.
3. The canonical `actinv-covariance-1` NPZ contains fixed-shape integer component descriptors plus deduplicated,
   offset-indexed binary64 energy grids and values. Its adjacent index records the activation-library/index hashes,
   source hashes, builder fingerprint, representations, target/component counts, coverage and NPZ hash. No path,
   hostname or timing enters canonical bytes. Writes are atomic; source/options/fingerprint-addressed target
   checkpoints are revalidated before reuse; one/four-worker and fresh/cached outputs are byte-identical.
4. MF=33 HEAD, subsection and LIST dimensions, tails, counts, energy order, finite values, LS/LT rules and the
   algebraic sizes of every LB representation are checked. P11 supports NI forms LB=0,1,2,3,4,5,6,8,9 as defined by
   ENDF-102. A foreign MAT, MF other than the File-3 reference, NC component, lumped MTL reaction, unknown LB,
   malformed list or unsupported cross-sublibrary reference fails that covariance target with MAT/MF/MT context;
   it is never ignored or converted to zero. The complete TENDL-2025 neutron corpus must require none of those
   unsupported forms.
5. Runtime validates the sidecar, its declared SHA-256, activation-library SHA-256, activation-index SHA-256,
   projectile, group boundaries and target/source identities before matrix assembly. An omitted uncertainty object
   reads no sidecar and preserves the pre-P11 result bytes.
6. Each covariance parameter is one activation-library row, identified by target ZA/LISO, MT, ZAP, LFS and LMF, and
   measured as that row's spectrum-collapsed cross section in barns. Loss and product rows remain separate
   parameters; their common MF=33 field supplies their covariance. MF=3-derived, MF=9 and MF=6 rows receive the
   MF=33 cross-section component using their own nominal group row. This does not invent uncertainty for the MF=9 or
   MF=6 yield. MF=10 rows are uncovered because their covariance belongs in MF=40, outside P11.
7. A row is numerically covered only when its target/MT has a valid MF=33 self-covariance. A missing cross component
   between two covered rows contributes zero as absent evaluated covariance and is counted explicitly. A missing
   self-covariance makes the row uncovered: it contributes no fabricated variance and appears in every affected
   result's coverage ledger. Reports say `complete` only when every nonzero sensitivity row is covered.
8. For a user spectrum, group-integrated flux is distributed uniformly in energy inside each activation group for
   covariance processing; the stored group cross section is constant on that group. The union of activation and
   covariance boundaries is used, so covariance-boundary crossings are never selected by a midpoint shortcut.
   Relative LB components multiply the corresponding row cross sections. Absolute LB=0 is used directly. LB=8 and
   LB=9 use ENDF-102's subgroup-width laws and produce diagonal short-range contributions. The one-group covariance
   is the double flux-weighted sum divided by total flux squared. Zero total flux gives zero collapsed cross sections
   and covariance.
9. No covariance value is clipped, decorrelated, projected to positive semidefinite form or otherwise repaired.
   Symmetry is checked after assembling both orientations. A materially negative propagated response variance is a
   hard error; a negative round-off residue no larger than `128*epsilon*sum(abs(S_i*C_ij*S_j))` is reported and set
   to zero. Evaluator-supplied non-positive-semidefinite structure remains visible in diagnostics.
10. `options.cram_order` accepts `16` or `48` and defaults to `16`, preserving historical bytes and behavior. Both
    orders use generated incomplete-partial-fraction coefficients. Uncertainty runs differentiate the selected
    CRAM recurrence itself: at each pole, `dz = M^-1 (dy - dt*dA*z)`, reusing the primal factorization for every
    sensitivity right-hand side. Pulse segments carry tangents across boundaries; zero-flux segments have `dA=0`.
11. Sensitivities are local derivatives at the nominal data and fixed selected trace/coupled mode, prune set and
    schedule. They are emitted for every covariance parameter with nonzero matrix influence, in response-units per
    barn, before any display threshold. Trace-mode bulk sources, coupled depletion, fission-yield expansion, leakage,
    pulses and cooling use the same row-to-matrix mapping as the nominal solve. The sensitivity calculation may not
    silently change ownership architecture or clone the complete prepared nuclear data per parameter.
12. The optional top-level spec object is:

    ```json
    "uncertainty": {
      "covariance": {"path": "library.cov.npz", "sha256": "64 hexadecimal digits"},
      "responses": ["heat.total", "heat.alpha", "heat.beta", "heat.gamma", "activity:Mn56"],
      "confidence_level": 0.95
    }
    ```

    Response selectors are unique and canonical; `activity:*` requests every reported activity. The default response
    set is the four heat components plus every reported activity. Confidence is finite and strictly between zero and
    one. P11 reports the nominal value, MF=33 standard uncertainty, relative standard uncertainty when defined, and
    the two-sided normal interval using the requested confidence. It also reports the alternate CRAM-order response
    difference as a separate numerical-method bound and a conservative interval expanded by that bound. It does not
    describe the normal interval as a tolerance limit or safety margin.
13. The full sensitivity records, collapsed covariance diagnostics, coverage, confidence multiplier, sidecar/source
    hashes, collapse convention, selected and comparison CRAM orders, excluded uncertainty sources and method name
    appear in results and certificates. CLI, PyO3, prepared and mesh entry points use the same prepared immutable
    sidecar. Mesh workers borrow it; they do not re-read or clone it per cell.

## Gates

**G1 Strict MF=33 parser and deterministic sidecar.** Independent Python and Rust parsers agree at zero ULP on all
retained numeric fields and exactly on all structure in the pinned Fe-56 and Ni-58 evaluations, including their
section/component/LB counts. Canonical one/four-worker and fresh/cached sidecars and indexes are byte-identical;
changing one source invalidates only that checkpoint. At least twelve plants covering a numeric field, LIST count,
energy order, LS/LT, unknown LB, NC, foreign MAT, wrong MF reference, duplicate target, source mutation, truncation
and activation/source-hash mismatch fail with context and publish no final pair. Peak RSS is below 2 GiB and no
single allocation reaches 1 GiB.

**G2 Covariance collapse and real-data reference.** Synthetic fixtures exercise LB=0--6,8,9, both LB=5 symmetry
forms, rectangular LB=6, boundary crossings and zero flux. Every collapsed row-pair value agrees with an independent
dense union-grid implementation within `2e-12` relative or `1e-16 barn^2` absolute. For pinned TENDL-2025 Fe-56,
ACTINV and the independent parser agree under three fixed nonnegative 709-group spectra to the same tolerance;
selected MT=102 self and cross-reaction matrices agree with a fresh NJOY2016.79 ERRORR calculation within `5e-3`
relative or `1e-14 barn^2` absolute. Orientation/transposition, symmetry diagnostics, source hashes and uncovered-row
counts are re-derived independently.

**G3 CRAM-48 and analytic sensitivities.** CRAM-16 and CRAM-48 both reproduce independent dense exponentials on
the existing analytic networks, with CRAM-48 also matching OpenMC 0.15.3 on resolvable populations within `1e-12`.
On trace and coupled two-reaction networks with irradiation, pulse and cooling segments, the differentiated CRAM
sensitivities of all four heat responses and both requested activities to every active collapsed row agree with
five-point finite differences within `1e-4` relative or the control's `1e-18` response-unit-per-barn absolute floor.
The selected mode/prune set does not change during the comparison, zero-flux `dA` is exact, and a no-uncertainty
legacy result is byte-identical after normalizing only the intentional solver-version leaf.

**G4 Propagated variance versus sampling.** On a positive-definite 2x2 cross-section covariance with a nonzero
off-diagonal term, direct `S C S^T`, the Rust propagated value and an independent dense calculation agree within
`1e-12`. A fixed-seed, chunked, antithetic normal sample of `2^26` linearized responses agrees with the propagated
variance within `1e-3` relative. Removing the cross term changes the result by the independently predicted amount.
Zero variance, perfect correlation and round-off-negative cases follow the frozen rules; a materially negative
variance, nonfinite component or mismatched dimension fails rather than producing a band.

**G5 Reports, certificates and entry points.** Every requested heat/activity response at every step carries the
nominal value, MF=33 standard uncertainty, requested normal interval, separate CRAM-order bound, conservative
expanded interval and complete/partial coverage. Every parameter sensitivity and unit is present. CLI, PyO3,
prepared and mesh paths have zero differing scientific/provenance fields after removing entry labels and timing.
All covariance/library/index/source hashes independently rematch. Wrong hashes, projectile/group/target/source
mismatch, duplicate selectors, bad confidence, unknown nuclide/response and an uncovered response planted as
`require_complete` fail before solving.

**G6 Complete corpus, regression and documentation.** The checkpointed Rust path scans all 2,850 external
TENDL-2025 neutron evaluations and builds the covariance sidecar with zero parse errors for every present MF=33
section and zero silently ignored components. Counts, coverage, representation inventory, source manifest,
fingerprint, fresh/cached hashes, resume profile and bounded memory are recorded; files without MF=33 and activation
rows without MF=33 are counted rather than invented. Workspace tests, strict rustfmt/Clippy, the CI subset and all
P5-P10 controls pass with their recorded verdicts. The specification, method, data, ledger, validation, release
limitations and CLI help state the collapse convention and excluded uncertainty sources without implying MF=32,
MF=40, flux/model uncertainty or a licensing safety margin.

# P145 Amendment C — the C3 covariance sidecars are generated, not tracked

Registered 2026-10-16, after the first evidence attempt failed U5 and before any repair. No criterion changes.

## What happened

The evidence on candidate `9f6b782` passed U1–U4 and the local half of U5, and five of the six workflows on the
pushed evidence commit `51a42cd` succeeded. The `controls` workflow failed in its step "P13 verified
data-distribution contract": `controls/g1_p13_data_distribution.py` refuses every tracked `.npz`, `.zip` or `.endf`
file that is not a reviewed archive, and the candidate tracked the three synthetic C3 covariance sidecars
`controls/p145_inputs/c3_p{10,50,90}.cov.npz`. U5 fails as written; `check_p145.py verdict` gave P145-FAIL. The
attempt is preserved in `results/failures/p145/` (`ci_attempt1/` and `evidence_attempt1/`). The local evidence ran
only the gates P145 names, not the P13 step, so the failure first appeared in CI.

## Repair

The sidecars are untracked and ignored. `controls/p145_c3_make.py` already writes them deterministically and
`controls/p145_inputs/c3_cases.json` and the C3 specs pin each one by SHA-256. `controls/check_p145.py` generates a
sidecar into the staging folder when it stages a C3 case and refuses to continue unless the generated bytes have the
pinned SHA-256, so U3 runs on byte-identical inputs. Its self-test generates them the same way. No other change: the
synthetic data are not added to the reviewed-archive list, which stays reserved for archives without evaluated data.

## Evidence after the repair

All of U1–U5 are rerun in full on the repaired candidate, with a fresh release binary and wheel, under the unchanged
criteria (Amendments A and B), and the P13 step and the rest of the `controls` workflow's cheap gates are run
locally before the push. This amendment consumes P145's one repair round.

## Why the phase fails

The frozen G2 rule compares every MF10 state partial and mutually exclusive state sum with the corresponding MF3
reaction total at every union-grid energy and after collapse; MF9 derived partials receive the same audit. It allows
only the exact predeclared absolute/relative excess. The full scan finds:

| projectile | files failing conservation | comparison violations | identity conflicts |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| neutron | 2,365 / 2,850 | 2,128,813 | 28 |
| proton | 2,411 / 2,850 | 175,883 | 44 |
| deuteron | 2,417 / 2,850 | 264,272 | 25 |
| alpha | 2,253 / 2,850 | 78,647 | 46 |

The source contains both small evaluated precision/domain discrepancies and large pointwise state-sum excesses. The
worst is neutron Nb-85m MT103 at `1.258925e-3 eV`, where the state sum is about 10.7 times the MF3 total. All 143
MF8-versus-Q conflicts remain explicit. Neither threshold, file inventory nor row accounting was changed after the
result. The independent G2 checker recomputes aggregate and worst-case arithmetic and rejects seven evidence
mutations while preserving `P18-G2-FAIL`.

Under the protocol, a failed threshold terminates the phase. Continuing to runtime integration, diagnostic scoring,
held-out unsealing, full artifact rebuilding or release would convert a known failed premise into a default, so those
gates were not run.

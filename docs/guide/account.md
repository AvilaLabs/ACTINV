# Avila Labs account (optional)

ACTINV can be used without an account, and every feature works without one. If you like, you can
sign in with an Avila Labs account. One sign-in works across ACTINV, Converra and OpenBNCT.

## Sign in

- **Browser workbench:** choose **Sign in** in the header, or answer the prompt shown on your first
  visit. You can dismiss the prompt and sign in later.
- **Desktop (0.3.0-preview.1 and later):** choose **Sign in**. The desktop shows a short code and
  opens your web browser, where you approve the sign-in. The desktop then continues by itself.

## What is sent and stored

Nothing from your work is sent: no problem inputs, results or files. The sign-in only identifies
your account.

The desktop stores its sign-in token in `~/.config/avila/credentials.json`. Choosing **Sign out**
revokes the token and removes it from that file.

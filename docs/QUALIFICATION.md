# Qualification boundary

ACTINV is research-grade software. Recorded controls do not approve the software or a calculation for licensing, safety, waste classification, or another regulatory purpose.

Choose a radiological table for the intended jurisdiction and scenario; ACTINV supplies no default regulatory selection. Verify the SHA-256 identities of the selected data and response tables. Assess validation applicability against the material, reactions, spectrum, and time range of the intended calculation.

The current capability boundaries, uncertainty exclusions, and analyst responsibilities are maintained in the [ACTINV Handbook — Scope and qualification](guide/qualification.md). Read [Known data limitations](guide/data-limits.md) and [Validation evidence](guide/validation.md) when assessing applicability.
